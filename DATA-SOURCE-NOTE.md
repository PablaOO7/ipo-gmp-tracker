## Data Source & Development Note

This is an independent, personal IPO tracking project created for informational and educational purposes.

Version 1 uses IPOWatch as its intended source for available IPO and Grey Market Premium (GMP) information. A future version may use free APIs or other permitted data sources.

GMP figures are unofficial, may be delayed or inaccurate, and are not guaranteed indicators of listing performance. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by IPOWatch. Source names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
